Raphinha is a dynamic Brazilian winger who has made a significant impact since joining FC Barcelona at 27. His impressive skills have earned him a reputation as one of the most exciting players globally. With a transfer value of just 54 million euros, he remains a key player for both club and country, showcasing his speed, precision crossing, and clinical finishing ability.

Raphinha has been a standout player this season, boasting an impressive average Sofascore rating of 8.16. His hat-trick and assist against Valladolid earned him a perfect 10.0 match rating, while notable performances in matches against Young Boys, Bayern Munich, Sevilla, and Villarreal resulted in ratings of 9.5, 9.2, 8.7, and 8.6 respectively, showcasing his consistent excellence on the pitch.
